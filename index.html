About Me

Bridging resilient enterprise architectures with frontier artificial intelligence.

I am a Software Engineer & Quality Assurance Specialist at HCLTech, currently dedicated to a strategic Microsoft client initiative focused on validating AI-driven capabilities and Agentic workflows.

My journey began in high-stakes enterprise banking infrastructure for the Commonwealth Bank of Australia. Over nearly three years, I engineered, maintained, and operated mission-critical batch processing systems in COBOL, JCL, DB2, and Java for consumer loans, passbook accounts, and home loan systems. Within the Microsoft project, I validate Copilot and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server agents, analyzing system behaviors, tool-calling pipelines, and prompt robustness across Teams, Outlook, Word, PowerPoint, and SharePoint.
