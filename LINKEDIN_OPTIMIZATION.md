## 4. How to Deploy Your Portfolio Website for Free (2 Minutes)

Your website files are located in:
`Documents/Future/portfolio/` (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, and `Akshay_Balachandran_Resume.pdf`).

### Option 1: GitHub Pages (Easiest)
1. Create a repository on GitHub named `akshay-portfolio` (or `balachandran-akshay.github.io`).
2. Push the files in `portfolio/` to the repo.
3. Go to **Settings > Pages > Branch: main / root > Save**.
4. Your website is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/`!

### Option 2: Vercel (Instant Drag-and-Drop)
1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com).
2. Click **Add New Project > Import** or drag and drop the `portfolio` folder.
3. Your site gets a free custom URL like `https://akshay-balachandran.vercel.app`.

## 5. Adding the Website to Your LinkedIn Profile

Once deployed:
1. **Header Link (Custom Button):**
   - Click the pencil icon on your LinkedIn intro.
   - Scroll down to **Custom Website / Link**.
   - Paste your portfolio URL and set text to: *"View My Portfolio & Projects ↗"*.
2. **Featured Section:**
   - Click **Add profile section > Recommended > Add Featured**.
   - Choose **Add a link** and paste your website URL.
   - LinkedIn will automatically generate an attractive preview card!
